# Privacy Policy - Critical Count

**Effective date:** September 28, 2026
**Developer:** Cloudy Day Games
**Contact:** cloudydaygamesllc@gmail.com

This policy explains what information is handled when you play **Critical Count** (Android package `com.cloudydaygames.criticalcount`), who handles it, and the choices you have.

## 1. The short version

- Critical Count plays fully offline. An internet connection is optional and is only used to show ads when one is available.
- We (Cloudy Day Games) do not run any servers for this game and do not collect, receive or sell your personal information.
- Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
- The free version shows ads served by **Google AdMob**. Google collects device and usage information to show those ads, as described below.
- If you buy **Remove Ads**, the payment is handled entirely by **Google Play**.

## 2. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device only. It is never sent to us:

- Your progress (stages cleared, cards collected, your current run)
- Your settings (volume, animations, battery saver)
- Whether you own Remove Ads, so the game remembers it offline

Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Critical Count shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ads are only requested when your device is online; without a connection the game simply plays without them. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

- Your device's advertising ID (the Android Advertising ID)
- Your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
- Device information such as model, operating system version and language
- Information about ad interactions, such as ads shown and tapped
- Diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data from the ad software

Google uses this information to serve and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and detect fraud and abuse. Depending on your choices and where you live, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

We limit ads in Critical Count to content rated suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (PG) and block sensitive ad categories such as gambling.

How Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. Your choices

- **Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland):** you are asked through Google's consent form, before any ad is requested, whether you agree to personalized advertising. You may decline.
- **US state privacy rights:** where applicable, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising.
- **Changing your mind:** where either of these applies, open **Options -> Privacy Choices** in the game at any time to review or withdraw your choices.
- **Advertising ID:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings -> Google -> Ads on most Android devices).
- **No ads at all:** buying Remove Ads stops all ads in the game.

## 5. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Remove Ads is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; we never see your payment details, name or email address. The game only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made, so it can switch ads off and restore the purchase on a new device.
Google Play's privacy terms: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 6. Children

Critical Count is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

## 7. Security and retention

We hold no personal information about you on any server. Data stored on your device stays there until you uninstall the game. Information collected by Google is kept according to Google's own policies, linked above.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information. Because we do not hold personal information about you, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google, but you are welcome to contact us and we will help.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, adding online features), we will update this policy and change the effective date above before the change ships.
